Privacy Policy

Isla Aiden Uploader, a tool of Hamboo (home page). Last updated: 23 November 2026.

What the app is

Isla Aiden Uploader is a private tool that the Hamboo team runs on its own computer to manage its own YouTube channel, "Hamboo: Learn Farsi for Kids with Isla & Aiden" (@HambooFarsi). It is not offered to the public, and only the Hamboo team's own Google accounts sign in to it.

What data it uses

With the permission given when we sign in with Google, the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • read the name of the signed-in YouTube channel, to confirm it is the Hamboo channel;
  • upload our own videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and publish times;
  • find, update or replace videos that we uploaded to our own channel.

The app does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube users.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token and a list of the videos the app has uploaded are stored only on the Hamboo team's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own services, and nothing is stored on this website.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer any data obtained through Google APIs to anyone. The app shows no ads. This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is removed, the app can no longer reach the channel, and we delete the stored sign-in token from our computer.

Children

Our videos are made for children, but the app itself is used only by the adults who run the Hamboo channel. It does not collect any information from children or other viewers.
