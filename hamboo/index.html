Hamboo: Learn Farsi for Kids with Isla & Aiden

آموزش زبان فارسی به کودکان با آیلا و ایدن

Hamboo is a family-made learning series for children aged 2 to 13. In each episode, Isla and Aiden go on a blocky animated adventure and learn new Farsi (Persian) words, with short daily videos that teach one word at a time.

Isla Aiden Uploader

Isla Aiden Uploader is a small private tool used only by the Hamboo team on our own computer. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload our own episodes and short videos to the Hamboo YouTube channel, schedule when they are published, and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions. It does not access anyone else's account or data, and it is not offered to the public.
